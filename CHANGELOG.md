# Changelog

## [1.0.0] - 2026-06-14

- Initial public Planetka release for Blender 4.5 LTS and newer supported Blender versions.
- Adds the simplified `Planetka by Tomas Griger` panel with Planetka Data status, edition, Quality Level, the combined Create New Earth / Resolve Planetka action, Diagnostics, and Links.
- Provides cloud-backed Earth texture resolving with Preview, Balanced, and Full quality modes.
- Provides Studio-only Quick Preview and Final Animation Render controls.
- Keeps Planetka focused on Earth creation, texture resolving, and Studio animation rendering.
