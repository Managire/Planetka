# Planetka Privacy Policy

Last updated: 2026-06-14

Planetka collects the minimum operational data needed to provide hosted data access, support, security, abuse prevention, and service reliability.

## Data We May Process

Planetka may process:

- anonymous install identifiers, device/session identifiers, and token metadata;
- Free, Hobby, Pro, or Studio service metadata where a paid service is arranged;
- payment or invoice metadata where a paid service is arranged;
- texture request metadata needed to serve data and protect the service;
- operational telemetry, errors, cache status, request counts, and bytes served;
- support messages you send to Planetka.

Planetka does not intentionally collect your Blender project files, scene contents, rendered images, private client work, or local files, except metadata needed to process Planetka texture requests and support issues.

## Payments

Planetka may provide Free access without payment. Hobby, Pro, Studio, higher usage allowance, custom support, or other written service arrangements may be processed by the payment provider, marketplace, invoice, or written agreement used for that service. Planetka does not store your full card number or full payment-card details.

## How Data Is Used

Planetka uses operational data to:

- provide Planetka Cloud access;
- deliver texture data;
- protect against scraping, abuse, fraud, and package sharing abuse;
- provide support and service assistance;
- maintain service reliability and analytics.

Planetka does not sell, rent, trade, or disclose usage, payment, telemetry, or support data to third parties for marketing, advertising, profiling, or independent use.

## Retention And Security

Planetka keeps operational, support, payment-status, and abuse-prevention records for as long as needed to provide access, support users, satisfy accounting requirements, protect the service, and resolve disputes.

Planetka uses reasonable technical and organisational measures to protect service data. No internet service can guarantee perfect security. You are responsible for keeping your local computer and Blender installation secure.

## Contact

For privacy, support, or data requests, contact Planetka through the support or contact address provided on planetka.io.
