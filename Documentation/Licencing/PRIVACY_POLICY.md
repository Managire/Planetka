# Planetka Privacy Policy

Last updated: 2026-05-13

This Privacy Policy explains what information Planetka collects and uses when you access Planetka-hosted services from the Blender add-on, Planetka web pages, checkout pages, or related account workflows.

## 1. Information Planetka collects

Planetka may collect and store:

- account email address and account status;
- API key records and authentication events;
- accepted Terms and Privacy Policy versions;
- product purchases, payment status, Stripe session identifiers, Stripe customer identifiers, and transaction metadata;
- licenced tiles, licenced data packs, purchase-history summaries, and entitlement records;
- tile requests, resolve estimates, download totals, data-downloaded totals, request counts, timing information, and service diagnostics;
- technical information needed for security, abuse prevention, debugging, and support, such as IP-derived request metadata, user agent, add-on version, Blender version, and error logs.

Planetka does not intentionally collect your Blender project files, scene contents, rendered images, private client work, or local files, except for metadata needed to process Planetka texture requests and support issues.

## 2. Payment information

Payments are processed by Stripe or another payment provider shown during checkout. Planetka does not store your full card number or full payment-card details.

When you choose to make a payment, payment details are entered directly into the payment provider's checkout flow and are handled under that provider's own terms and privacy policy. Planetka stores only the payment status and metadata needed to connect the payment to your Planetka account and entitlements.

## 3. How Planetka uses information

Planetka uses collected information to:

- authenticate users and API keys;
- provide Preview texture access and licenced Full Quality texture access;
- calculate prices, discounts, deductions, and purchase eligibility;
- create checkout sessions and confirm successful purchases;
- record purchase history and licenced texture entitlements;
- support downloads of licenced data;
- measure service reliability, performance, usage, and download volume;
- prevent abuse, unauthorised access, scraping, payment misuse, and excessive automated requests;
- provide customer support, invoice support, and account troubleshooting;
- comply with legal, tax, accounting, security, and operational obligations.

## 4. No data sharing

Planetka does not sell, rent, trade, disclose, or share account, usage, purchase, entitlement, telemetry, or support data with third parties. Planetka does not provide user email addresses or usage data to third parties for marketing, advertising, profiling, analytics, or independent use.

Data stays in Planetka-controlled systems and is used only to operate Planetka, provide account access, deliver texture data, record purchases and entitlements, provide support, secure the service, and maintain required operational records.

## 5. Retention

Planetka keeps account, purchase, entitlement, and payment-status records for as long as needed to provide licenced data access, prove purchase history, support users, satisfy accounting requirements, and resolve disputes.

Operational logs, diagnostics, and aggregated usage analytics may be retained for shorter periods or summarised over time unless needed for security, fraud prevention, support, or legal reasons.

## 6. Security

Planetka uses reasonable technical and organisational measures to protect account and service data. No internet service can guarantee perfect security. You are responsible for keeping your Planetka account, API key, local computer, and Blender installation secure.

## 7. Your choices and rights

You may contact Planetka to request access, correction, export, or deletion of personal information where applicable by law. Some information, such as purchase history, entitlement records, payment metadata, and legal/accounting records, may need to be retained even if other account information is deleted.

## 8. Data location and processing

Planetka data is kept in Planetka-controlled systems. Some hosted infrastructure may physically operate in data centres outside your country of residence, but Planetka does not share your account, usage, purchase, entitlement, telemetry, or support data with third parties for their own use.

## 9. Changes

Planetka may update this Privacy Policy as the product, payment model, legal requirements, or service infrastructure changes. The updated policy will show a new last-updated date.

## 10. Contact

For privacy, account, or data requests, contact Planetka through the support or contact address provided on [planetka.io](https://www.planetka.io).
