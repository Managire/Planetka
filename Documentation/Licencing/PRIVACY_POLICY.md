# Planetka Privacy Policy

Last updated: 2026-05-19

This Privacy Policy explains what information Planetka collects and uses when you access Planetka-hosted services from the Blender add-on, Planetka web pages, checkout pages, or related account workflows.

## 1. Information Planetka collects

Planetka may collect and store:

- account email address and account status;
- access-key records and authentication events;
- accepted Terms and Privacy Policy versions;
- account type, such as Personal or Professional;
- payment status, payment-provider session identifiers, customer identifiers, and transaction metadata when a payment is made;
- tile requests, quality-mode requests, cache/session metadata, request counts, timing information, and service diagnostics;
- technical information needed for security, abuse prevention, debugging, and support, such as IP-derived request metadata, user agent, add-on version, Blender version, and error logs.

Planetka does not intentionally collect your Blender project files, scene contents, rendered images, private client work, or local files, except for metadata needed to process Planetka texture requests and support issues.

## 2. Payment information

Payments, where offered, are processed by Stripe or another payment provider shown during checkout. Planetka does not store your full card number or full payment-card details.

When you choose to make a payment, payment details are entered directly into the payment provider's checkout flow and are handled under that provider's own terms and privacy policy. Planetka stores only the payment status and metadata needed to connect the payment to your Planetka account.

## 3. How Planetka uses information

Planetka uses collected information to:

- authenticate users and access keys;
- provide Personal or Professional Planetka streaming access;
- enforce selected Personal free locations and Professional worldwide access;
- create checkout sessions and confirm successful payments where Professional checkout is offered;
- measure service reliability, performance, usage, and streamed data volume;
- prevent abuse, unauthorised access, scraping, payment misuse, and excessive automated requests;
- provide customer support, invoice support, and account troubleshooting;
- comply with legal, tax, accounting, security, and operational obligations.

## 4. No data sharing

Planetka does not sell, rent, trade, disclose, or share account, usage, payment, telemetry, or support data with third parties. Planetka does not provide user email addresses or usage data to third parties for marketing, advertising, profiling, analytics, or independent use.

Data stays in Planetka-controlled systems and is used only to operate Planetka, provide account access, deliver texture data, provide support, secure the service, and maintain required operational records.

## 5. Retention

Planetka keeps account, account-access, payment-status, and legal-consent records for as long as needed to provide service access, support users, satisfy accounting requirements, and resolve disputes.

Operational logs, diagnostics, and aggregated usage analytics may be retained for shorter periods or summarised over time unless needed for security, fraud prevention, support, or legal reasons.

## 6. Security

Planetka uses reasonable technical and organisational measures to protect account and service data. No internet service can guarantee perfect security. You are responsible for keeping your Planetka account, access key, local computer, and Blender installation secure.

## 7. Your choices and rights

You may contact Planetka to request access, correction, export, or deletion of personal information where applicable by law. Some information, such as payment metadata, legal-consent records, and legal/accounting records, may need to be retained even if other account information is deleted.

## 8. Data location and processing

Planetka data is kept in Planetka-controlled systems. Some hosted infrastructure may physically operate in data centres outside your country of residence, but Planetka does not share your account, usage, payment, telemetry, or support data with third parties for their own use.

## 9. Changes

Planetka may update this Privacy Policy as the product, payment model, legal requirements, or service infrastructure changes. The updated policy will show a new last-updated date.

## 10. Contact

For privacy, account, or data requests, contact Planetka through the support or contact address provided on [planetka.io](https://www.planetka.io).
