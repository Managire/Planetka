# Planetka Privacy Policy

Last updated: 2026-03-26

This Privacy Policy explains what information Planetka collects in connection
with Planetka accounts and Planetka-hosted services used by the Blender add-on.

## 1. Information Collected

Planetka may collect:

- your email address
- account identifiers and login/session state
- service usage metrics
- performance metrics
- account-related metrics
- technical request metadata needed for security and service operation

Examples may include:

- sign-in events
- resolve/download activity totals
- request timing and performance diagnostics
- device/browser/request metadata needed to detect abuse or keep the service
  stable

The only user-provided personal identifier Planetka collects is your email
address. Planetka does not request or store your name, postal address, or
telephone number for account access or normal add-on usage.

Planetka does not use this information to sell personal profiles or to provide
advertising to third parties.

## 2. How Information Is Used

Planetka uses collected information to:

- authenticate users
- operate Planetka-hosted services
- monitor stability and performance
- detect abuse, scraping, or policy violations
- troubleshoot failures
- improve the service internally

## 3. No Sale; No Third-Party Sharing for Marketing

Planetka does not sell personal data.

Planetka does not share account, usage, or performance data with third parties
for their own advertising or marketing purposes.

## 4. Limited Sharing

Planetka may share information only when reasonably necessary to:

- operate hosting, storage, email, security, and analytics infrastructure
- comply with legal obligations
- investigate abuse or protect the service

## 5. Retention

Planetka may retain account, security, and service-operation records for as
long as reasonably necessary to operate the service, investigate abuse, comply
with legal obligations, or maintain backups and logs.

## 6. Security

Planetka uses reasonable technical and operational measures to protect account
and service data, but no system can be guaranteed to be perfectly secure.

## 7. Your Choices

If you want to ask about your account data or request account deletion, contact:

- info@planetka.io
- https://www.planetka.io/contact-me

Planetka may keep certain records where reasonably required for security,
abuse-prevention, backup, or legal compliance.

## 8. Changes

Planetka may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. The updated version
applies after publication.

## 9. Contact

For privacy questions, contact:

- info@planetka.io
- https://www.planetka.io/contact-me
