Using Planetka Commercially

Planetka has two single-seat licence options:

1. Personal Licence. This licence is for personal, educational, evaluation, portfolio, and other non-commercial use only.
2. Commercial Licence. This licence is required for paid client work, studio work, advertising, broadcast, commercial products, commercial services, and any other commercial use.

Both licences include the same Planetka add-on functionality and the same access to Planetka-hosted data. Preview, Balanced, and Full quality modes are available under both licences.

The only difference is the legal usage right. If the work is commercial, the user must have a Commercial Licence.

Planetka is not a raw-data download product. You may not resell, redistribute, sublicense, host, publish, extract, or expose Planetka texture data as a standalone map product, dataset, tile server, API, texture library, training dataset, or competing service.
