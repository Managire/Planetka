Using Planetka Commercially
Last updated: 2026-05-12

1. Preview texture access is free, streamed/cached, and personal / non-commercial only.
2. Full Quality texture data includes a commercial licence for the Full Quality data licenced to your account.
3. Full Quality can be licenced through a scene-specific purchase, animation purchase, promotional grant, or predefined data pack.
4. Full Quality data that has been licenced to your account can be reused without being charged again where Planetka's current product rules support that reuse.
5. Supported Full Quality downloads may be used in Blender or in other creative/production tools, subject to the Terms of Service and source-attribution requirements.
6. You may not resell, redistribute, sublicense, host, publish, or expose Planetka texture data as a standalone map product, dataset, tile server, API, texture library, training dataset, or competing service.
7. You may not use Preview textures for paid client work, broadcast work, commercial production, resale, dataset extraction, or any workflow outside the intended Planetka preview workflow.
8. You must include required source attribution where applicable. The recommended short attribution is:
   Planetka Earth textures derived from Copernicus Sentinel-2, NASA Blue Marble, JAXA AW3D30, DLR WSF 2015, GeoNames, and other credited sources.
9. For enterprise, broadcast, aerospace, large-documentary, AI/ML, data-redistribution, or unusual licensing requirements, contact Planetka before use.
