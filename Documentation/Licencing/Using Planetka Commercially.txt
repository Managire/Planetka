Using Planetka Commercially
Last updated: 2026-05-19

1. Free accounts are intended for evaluation and personal use worldwide in Preview and Balanced texture quality.
2. Pro accounts allow commercial and non-commercial rendered output created with Planetka streamed textures.
3. Preview, Balanced, and Full Quality are streaming quality modes, not separate tile purchases.
4. The Planetka add-on is not a raw-data download product and does not provide a reusable texture-data archive.
5. You may not resell, redistribute, sublicense, host, publish, extract, or expose Planetka texture data as a standalone map product, dataset, tile server, API, texture library, training dataset, or competing service.
6. You may not use Free access for paid client or commercial production.
7. You must include required source attribution where applicable. The recommended short attribution is:
   Planetka Earth textures derived from Copernicus Sentinel-2, NASA Blue Marble, JAXA AW3D30, DLR WSF 2015, GeoNames, and other credited sources.
8. For enterprise, broadcast, aerospace, large-documentary, AI/ML, data-redistribution, or unusual licensing requirements, contact Planetka before use.
