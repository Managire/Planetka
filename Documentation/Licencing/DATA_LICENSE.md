# Planetka Data Licence

Planetka texture data is a processed visualisation dataset made from Planetka-authored processing, retouching, optimisation, and third-party source datasets.

Planetka grants access to Planetka texture data only through Planetka Cloud and authorised Planetka editions, subject to the Planetka Terms of Service and Fair Usage Policy.

Planetka data may not be scraped, bulk-downloaded, redistributed, repackaged, exposed as a standalone texture library, map product, tile service, API, training dataset, or competing service.

This licence does not remove or replace upstream source-data obligations. Planetka data includes modified derivatives of datasets including:

- Copernicus Sentinel-2 imagery.
- JAXA AW3D30 (ALOS World 3D - 30m) elevation data.
- DLR World Settlement Footprint 2015 (WSF2015 v2) data.

- `Documentation/Licencing/Attribution for User Renders.txt`
- `Documentation/Licencing/Compliance/Earth Surface Data Sources.txt`

Rendered output may be used according to the user's Planetka edition and the Planetka Terms of Service. Public use may still require source-data attribution.

Planetka data is intended for cinematic visualisation. It is not suitable for safety-critical use, legal boundary determination, navigation, engineering decisions, emergency response, or any other use requiring authoritative geospatial accuracy.

For unrestricted access, API access to the texture database, or custom arrangements, contact info@planetka.io.
